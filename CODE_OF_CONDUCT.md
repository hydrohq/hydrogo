# Code of Conduct

HydroGo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages) and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **info@ardalabs.dev**. Reports are handled privately and in good faith. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).

This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
